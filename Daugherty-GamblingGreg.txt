Pseudocode

Greg starts with $100 cash and $500 in the bank

The program displays the menu with Gregs cash and bank balance:
    [G]amble, [B]anking, [R]esults of each race, [L]eave the dog track

Ask Greg for his choice:

    If
        when the choice is G then
        Ask Greg for his wager

        If
            when the wager is less than 0 or more than his cash then
            Tell Greg the bet is invalid

        Else
            Display the dogs with their payout and odds
            

            If
                when the pick is less than 1 or greater than 9 then
                Tell Greg the choice is invalid

            Else
                Generate a random number from 1 to 100
                Display the winner
                Save the pick, winner, and wager in the race log

                If
                    when the pick is the winner then
                    Add wager times payout to Gregs cash

                Else
                    Subtract the wager from Gregs cash

    Else If
        when the choice is B then
        Ask Greg to withdraw or deposit and how much


    Else If
        when the choice is R then
        Display the pick, winner, and wager of each saved race

    Else If
        when the choice is L then
        Display that the program ended

    Else
        Tell Greg that is not a choice

Check if Greg chose to leave:

    If
        when the choice is not L then
        repeat the menu with the updated cash, bank, and race count

    Else
        stop repeating

When the program exits:
    Convey that the program has ended.
